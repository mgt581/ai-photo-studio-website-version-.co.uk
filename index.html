Questions

Is AI Photo Studio free?

Yes. Background removal is free and unlimited, and every new account gets 3 free Remove Person credits. Free downloads carry a small watermark; Pro removes it and unlocks unlimited fair-use Remove Person and HD exports.

How do I remove a person from a photo?

Open the editor, upload your photo, tap Remove Person, paint over the person (including their shadow) and tap Apply Removal. The AI fills the area with matching background in about 15–25 seconds.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer, including iPhone. There's also an Android app on Google Play.

Are my photos private?

Background removal runs on your own device, so the photo isn't uploaded. For Remove Person, only the part of the photo around your selection is sent securely to our AI provider to create the edit. Saving to the cloud gallery is optional. See our privacy policy.

How do I cancel Pro?

Cancel any time using the link in your Stripe receipt email, or email info@aiphotostudio.co.uk. The yearly plan's 7-day trial is free if you cancel before it ends.

What works best with Remove Person?

Paint generously over the whole object and its shadow. Busy backgrounds like brick, grass, sky and streets give the most natural results; very large objects covering most of the photo are harder for any AI.
