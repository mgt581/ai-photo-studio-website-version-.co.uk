Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

At AI Photo Studio (aiphotostudio.co.uk and the "AI Background Remover" Android app), we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information and images.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Your email address and unique user ID when you create an account via Google or Email sign-in, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • User Content: Photos you upload for background removal or editing, and edits you save to your gallery.
  • Purchase Information: Your plan, credits and purchase history (e.g., Free vs. Pro). We never see or store your full card details.
  • Usage Data and Device Identifiers: How you use the app (for example which features are used) and device or app identifiers, collected through Firebase Analytics, push notifications and app security checks. We use this to improve the app and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Your Images

Your images are used solely for the purpose of providing the editing service (removing backgrounds, erasing objects, etc.).

  • Background removal runs on your own device, so the photo is not uploaded for that feature.
  • Remove Person / object removal: the part of the photo around your selection is sent securely to our AI processing providers (listed below) to create the edit, and the result is returned to you.
  • Processing: Images are processed securely. We do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • Storage: If you are signed in, processed images can be saved to your private Gallery so you can download them later. You can delete your account to remove all data.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to help run our service. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase: For secure authentication, database storage, file hosting, analytics (Firebase Analytics) and notifications.
  • Google Play: For in-app purchases and subscriptions in the Android app.
  • Stripe: For processing subscription payments on the website securely. We do not store your credit card details.
  • Cloudflare: For website hosting, security and AI image processing.
  • fal.ai: For AI object removal (Remove Person).

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and data at any time at aiphotostudio.co.uk/delete-account, or by emailing us at the address below.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Access to your gallery is restricted to your specific user account via secure authentication rules.
