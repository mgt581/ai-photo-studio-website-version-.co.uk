Cut out a person, pet or product in one tap. The background remover is free and unlimited, and it runs right on your device, so your photo is never uploaded.

Remove a background now

How to remove a background

  1. Open the editor and tap Choose Photo.
  2. Tap Remove Background. The first time, the AI model takes a few seconds to load; after that it's almost instant.
  3. Keep it transparent, or tap Background Color to add white or any colour.
  4. Download your PNG.

What it's great for

  • Product photos for Vinted, eBay, Etsy, Depop and Shopify — see our white-background guide.
  • Profile pictures for LinkedIn, CVs and social media.
  • Stickers, logos and graphics with transparent backgrounds.
  • Clean, bold colours for thumbnails and posters.

Private by design

Most online background removers upload your photo to a server. AI Photo Studio's background remover runs inside your browser using your device's processor, so the photo stays with you. (Only the separate Remove Person tool uses our cloud AI.)

Free vs Pro

Background removal is free and unlimited for everyone. Free downloads include a small watermark; Pro gives watermark-free HD exports, unlimited fair-use Remove Person and a cloud gallery.

Need to remove something from the photo instead?

If you want to keep the background and take a person or object out of it, use Remove Person.
