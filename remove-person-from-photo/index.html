How to remove a person from a photo

A stranger walked through your holiday shot, or you'd rather not see your ex in that group photo? You can erase anyone from a picture in under a minute — just paint over them and let AI rebuild the background.

Remove a person now — free
BeforeAfter

Real result from AI Photo Studio's Remove Person tool

Step by step

  1. Open the editor at aiphotostudio.co.uk/app (or the Android app) and tap Choose Photo.
  2. Tap Remove Person. A brush appears.
  3. Paint over the whole person — include their shadow, bag or anything they're holding. It's better to paint a little too much than too little.
  4. Tap Apply Removal and wait 15–25 seconds while the AI fills in the background.
  5. Check the result, then download it or save it to your cloud gallery.

Tips for the most natural result

  • Cover the shadow too. A leftover shadow is the most common giveaway.
  • Do big jobs in two passes. For crowds, remove one or two people at a time.
  • Busy backgrounds hide edits well. Grass, brick, sky, water and streets blend best.
  • Something still visible? Paint over just that bit and apply again.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. AI Photo Studio runs in Safari and Chrome, so it works on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers without installing anything. Android users can also get the app on Google Play.

Is it free?

Every account gets 3 free Remove Person credits, and background removal is free and unlimited. If you edit lots of photos, Pro gives you unlimited fair-use removals and watermark-free HD downloads from £1 for a day pass.

What else can I remove?

Anything you can paint over: cars, bins, signs, power lines, litter, tourists, logos on clothing and clutter in room photos. For cutting out the main subject instead, use the free background remover.
