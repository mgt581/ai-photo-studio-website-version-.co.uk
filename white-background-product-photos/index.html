White-background product photos for Vinted, eBay & Etsy

Listings with clean, bright photos get more clicks. Turn a quick phone snap on your bed or floor into a professional white-background product photo in under a minute — free.

Make a product photo

How to do it

  1. Take the photo in daylight near a window. Fill most of the frame with the item.
  2. Open the editor, upload it and tap Remove Background.
  3. Tap Background Color and pick white (or a soft grey or brand colour).
  4. Download and upload it to your listing.

Clean up the rest of the shot

Prefer to keep the real setting — a hanger, a room, a shelf? Use Remove Person to paint out clutter like price tags, cables, your hand or a pet that wandered into shot, while keeping the rest of the photo untouched.

Tips that help items sell

  • Shoot several angles — front, back, label and any flaws. Buyers trust honest photos.
  • Keep backgrounds consistent across your shop for a professional look.
  • Square crops work well on Vinted, Depop and Instagram.
  • Check marketplace rules: eBay recommends white or light-grey backgrounds for the main photo; Etsy and Vinted allow lifestyle shots too.
